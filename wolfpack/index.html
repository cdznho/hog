01 / Strategy overview

A local-intent wedge with a clear route to authority.

The strongest opportunity is not generic “fitness” content. It is a local service system around CrossFit, gym membership, HYROX, and beginner-friendly group training in Querétaro. Wolfpack has validated demand, useful program pages, two branch pages, and existing SERP visibility. The constraint is that large aggregators and chains own much of the broad “near me” surface, so the first 90 days should strengthen local entity signals and publish pages that answer price, fit, location, schedule, and beginner questions.

05 / LLM SEO and GEO

Make the entity easy for an answer engine to repeat.

Wolfpack's site already exposes a crawlable sitemap, canonical URLs, strong page titles/descriptions, a homepage WebSite and ExerciseGym schema block, and source-visible headings. The next layer is to express the entity and its programs consistently on every important page, then repeat that same language across trusted local surfaces.

Present

Crawl and index foundation

Robots: accessible and links to the sitemap. Sitemap: 15 URLs. Canonicals: observed on homepage, Metcon, and HYROX. Metadata: program-specific titles and descriptions.

Priority gap

Page-level entity depth

Only the homepage schema was observed in the sampled pages. Add LocalBusiness/ExerciseGym and Service or Course-like program markup where valid, plus FAQ blocks, branch details, coach identity, and clear last-updated signals.

Measurement

Prove the funnel

Confirm GA4 and Search Console baselines, then track intro-class clicks, WhatsApp/phone taps, booking starts, booking completions, branch selection, and branded vs. non-branded queries.

06 / 90-day execution plan

Build proof, then expand the cluster.

Weeks 1–2 · Fix the conversion and entity layer

Confirm the canonical strategy for crossfitqueretaro.com. Add consistent NAP details, phone/WhatsApp/book-intro events, branch links, FAQ sections, breadcrumbs, and page-level schema. Make pricing, schedule, beginner fit, and next step visible on every program page.

Weeks 2–4 · Strengthen the two local landing pages

Improve /locations/tec and /locations/5-de-febrero with unique branch copy, parking/arrival context, class timetable, coach proof, photos, reviews, and a direct intro-class CTA. Add a CrossFit Querétaro hub that links to both locations and Metcon/HYROX.

Weeks 4–8 · Publish the highest-value answers

Sequence: CrossFit Querétaro hub; gym for beginners in Querétaro; what HYROX is and how to prepare; CrossFit vs gym; first-class guide; pricing and membership FAQ. Keep each page tied to a real Wolfpack conversion.

Weeks 8–12 · Earn local repetition

Update Google Business Profile for each branch, collect review language around CrossFit, HIIT, HYROX, and beginner experience, publish short video answers, and seek local editorial/community mentions. Re-run the query set monthly and log AI citations.

Do not lead with: gym-near-me content pages, generic workout articles, or zero-volume program slugs. Use those phrases as supporting language inside stronger local pages.
